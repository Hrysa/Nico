# Library 2 animation selection

Historical validation of the initial migration. Hit playback was subsequently
removed; see the [Bestiary follow-up](2026-09-17-bestiary.md#follow-up-remove-presentation-only-injury).
Current bindings are documented in the [character contract](../plans/2026-09-17-character-definitions.md).

The hero now selects `Sword_Regular_C` from the locally downloaded Universal
Animation Library 2 Standard archive. The `ual2` profile maps its named humanoid
bones onto the existing Mixamo hero. The clip is extracted without mesh data;
hierarchy, channels, accessors, and animation sample bytes were compared against
the source GLB and preserved.

The contact marker is 0.686666667 seconds of a two-second clip. At the sampled
marker, the blade tip is approximately (-0.091, 0.742, 1.640) metres relative to
the standing hero: within the existing target radius at the two-metre attack
range. Simulation timing, damage, collision, and reach are unchanged.

The Standard archive's 43 clips do not replace the existing sword idle, normal
run, roll, or death. Those remain fallbacks. `Hit_Knockback` was tested and
rejected: with the existing equipped grip, a sampled blade vertex falls below
the floor at about 0.253 seconds. The existing hit reaction is retained.

  executed five attacks through bridge MCP. GPU captures and separately sampled

  (that scratch directory was later reused for Bestiary validation). Capture
